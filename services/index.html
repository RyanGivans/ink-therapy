What we offer

01 / Consults

Consultations

Most tattoo appointments begin with a consult so the artist can understand the idea, placement, size, references, skin flow, and meaning behind the piece.

Available in person or over video chat.
Typically 15–30 minutes.
Great for custom work, coverups, placement questions, and planning.
$50 deposit to book
02 / Appointments

Tattoo Appointments

Custom tattoo sessions are priced by the artist based on style, placement, complexity, size, and time required.

Pieces under two hours are usually priced by the piece.
Tattoos over two hours are generally priced hourly.
Each artist handles pricing based on their work and process.

How it works

A calm process from idea to appointment.

The goal is simple: make sure every client feels heard, prepared, and matched with the right artist for the piece.

1. Send the idea

Share your concept, placement, approximate size, style references, and preferred artist.

2. Consult when needed

For most tattoos, the artist will use a consult to shape the concept and prepare the appointment.

3. Book with deposit

A $50 deposit reserves the appointment and goes toward the tattoo process.

4. Arrive ready

Come hydrated, fed, rested, and ready to be cared for in a clean, inclusive studio.
